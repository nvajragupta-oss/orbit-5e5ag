## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-16

First release.

- 16-level "Space Math 1" pack for ages 7–8+:
  - add and subtract within 20
  - missing numbers
  - place value
  - comparing numbers
  - skip counting
  - telling the time
  - 2-digit adding and subtracting, with and without regrouping
  - tens and hundreds
  - equal groups
  - ×2/5/10 and ×3/4 tables
  - sharing
  - two-step story problems
- Every level goes from easier to harder, has 10 questions, and offers hints, a second try and step-by-step explanations.
- Stars (★–★★★) unlock the next planet. Several players each have their own progress.
- Read-aloud with subtitles, generated sound effects, and a space theme with no image files.
- Works offline (service worker) and can be installed on iPhone, iPad, Android and desktop.
- Grown-ups page: install help, voice/sound settings, player management, version and update check.
